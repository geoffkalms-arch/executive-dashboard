Kanban tub label generator

Prints onto polyester label sheets or plain card for label holders. Black-only, no bleeds, no background fills — designed so toner coverage stays low and edges stay crisp.

Stock & layout

Print one sheet on plain paper first and hold it against a label sheet up to the light. If it’s off, correct it with the nudge fields — most laser printers drift 1–2 mm. Set your print dialog to Scale: 100% (not “Fit to page”), or the barcodes will be out of spec.

Data

One label per line, fields separated by | (pipe) or tab — paste straight from a spreadsheet. Columns: part | description | location | qty | card | of | tubID | colour. Trailing columns can be left off. Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored.
