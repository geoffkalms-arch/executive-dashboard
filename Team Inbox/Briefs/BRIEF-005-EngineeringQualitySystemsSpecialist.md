## 1. Role Summary

The Engineering Quality Systems Specialist owns the management systems that govern how engineering and technical work is produced, reviewed, approved, and improved across The Works. This is not an engineering role and not a regulatory compliance role — it sits between those disciplines, ensuring that the processes used by Harlow, Petra, and Remy are documented, controlled, auditable, and continuously improved.

The core function is ISO 9001:2015 quality management system design, implementation, and maintenance in a manufacturing and engineering context specific to the Australian transport sector — heavy vehicles, dangerous goods plant, and related fabrication and assembly operations. The person in this role does not design structures, assess pressure systems, or interpret DG legislation. They build and maintain the systems that ensure those activities are performed, recorded, and verified in a controlled and traceable manner.

They will operate as an internal QMS custodian, interface with Harlow on engineering document control and design verification workflows, interface with Petra on process documentation and P&ID revision control, and interface with Remy on compliance record management and audit-readiness. They will also manage supplier quality assurance where subcontracted fabrication or materials supply is involved.

## 3. Day-to-Day Tasks

- Maintaining and updating the QMS document register — ensuring all procedures, work instructions, forms, and templates are at current revision and accessible
- Processing engineering change requests — coordinating with Harlow and Petra to update controlled drawings, P&IDs, and specifications through the approved change workflow
- Raising and tracking non-conformance reports (NCRs) — from identification through root cause analysis to corrective action close-out
- Running and recording internal audits against ISO 9001 and applicable AS/NZS requirements — scheduling audit calendar, preparing checklists, writing audit reports, tracking findings to closure
- Managing the approved supplier list — onboarding new suppliers, scheduling and conducting supplier audits, maintaining supplier performance records
- Preparing management review packs — compiling KPI data, audit results, customer feedback, NCR trends, and CAPA status
- Coordinating incoming inspection records — ensuring material certificates, test reports, and conformance documentation are received, verified, and filed against job numbers
- Maintaining training and competency records for personnel performing quality-critical tasks
- Supporting Remy on audit-readiness — ensuring compliance records are filed, indexed, and retrievable in a format suitable for regulatory or third-party audit
- Drafting or updating work instructions when process changes are identified by Harlow or Petra
- Monitoring corrective and preventive action (CAPA) registers — chasing owners, verifying effectiveness, closing out actions

## 5. Typical Credentials

**Formal Qualifications**

- Degree or diploma in Engineering, Quality Management, or Manufacturing Technology — or a trade background with substantial quality systems experience
- ISO 9001:2015 Lead Auditor certification (RABQSA / Exemplar Global accredited) — strongly preferred; internal auditor certification acceptable at minimum
- Certificate IV in Quality Auditing (BSB40920) or equivalent — relevant for candidates from a vocational pathway

**Experience Benchmarks**

- Minimum 5 years in a quality systems role within a manufacturing environment; heavy industry, heavy vehicle, or dangerous goods manufacturing strongly preferred
- Demonstrated experience owning a certified ISO 9001 QMS — not just contributing to one
- Experience conducting internal audits and managing NCR/CAPA systems to closure
- Experience interfacing with engineers on document control for technical deliverables (drawings, specifications, calculations)
- Experience with supplier quality management including supplier audits

**Desirable but Not Required**

- ISO 14001 (Environmental) or ISO 45001 (OHS) integrated management system experience
- Familiarity with AS/NZS standards used in the transport/DG sector
- Experience supporting third-party certification audits (BSI, SAI Global, Bureau Veritas, or equivalent)
- Exposure to configuration management in an engineering project environment

## 6. Sub-Specialisations — Worth Noting

**Integrated Management Systems (IMS)**
Some candidates will have built or maintained systems that combine ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001 into a single integrated framework. If Petra's WorkCover and DG plant compliance obligations expand, an IMS-capable person becomes significantly more valuable.

**Supplier Quality Engineering**
A sub-set of candidates will have a deeper supplier development background — running formal supplier development programs, source inspection, and incoming quality control beyond basic approved supplier lists. Useful given that fabrication subcontracting is likely in Harlow's scope.

**Configuration Management in Defence or Aerospace**
Candidates from defence or aerospace bring rigorous configuration management disciplines (AS 9100, MIL-STD) that transfer well to engineering document control in heavy vehicle and DG manufacturing.

**AS/NZS Standards Familiarity**
Candidates who have worked in Australian heavy vehicle manufacturing (tankers, tippers, refrigerated trailers) or dangerous goods plant fabrication will have direct exposure to AS 2809, AS 1210, and the ADG Code as quality framework drivers. This is a genuine differentiator — it reduces onboarding time significantly.

## 7. Felix's Notes

**What this person does NOT do**

- Does not interpret or apply the ADG Code, NHVR regulations, or WorkCover instruments — that is Remy's domain.
- Does not perform engineering design, structural assessment, or technical review of drawings — that is Harlow's domain.
- Does not assess P&ID design, instrument selection, or process safety — that is Petra's domain.
- Is not a quality inspector or production supervisor — this is a systems role.

**Interface mapping**

1. Harlow → QMS Specialist: Engineering drawings, calculation packages, design review records, and test reports flow into the QMS for controlled filing, revision management, and auditability.
2. Petra → QMS Specialist: P&IDs, piping specs, and instrument datasheets sit in the controlled document system. The QMS Specialist manages revision history and change workflow.
3. Remy → QMS Specialist: Compliance records need to be filed, indexed, and retrievable for audit. The QMS Specialist owns the filing architecture — does not assess content.

**Hiring priority signal**

The absence of this role creates compounding risk as the team's output volume grows. Harlow, Petra, and Remy are each producing controlled technical documents without a dedicated custodian managing revision control, change records, and audit trails. This hire is structural, not supplementary.

**Suggested screening question for Nolan**

Ask candidates: "Describe a situation where an engineering drawing was revised after it had already been released and used in fabrication. Walk me through how you managed the change — what records you raised, how you notified affected parties, and how you closed out the non-conformance."
