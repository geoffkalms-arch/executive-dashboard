STAGE 3 — New Chassis Build Step 1
MECHANICAL
75
 
 
 
🚫 NO grinder use allowed while equipment is on chassis
Start subframe fabrication from 75×75 SHS — seam facing inside or bottom of rails
Place subframe rails on chassis — cabin end to go OVER first spring hanger
Cut subframe cabin end: 45° with 15mm landing
Mark holes from chassis to subframe for rivets — cut out with plasma cutter
Fit subframe mounting plates to subframe and tack fit
Measure and cut cross members — tack in position
Mark out positions: tank, cabinet, mudguards, toolbox
Lift tank onto chassis — CAREFUL with paint/stainless finish
Using square: ensure tank is even and correctly located
Mark rear tank mount position — as close to rear axle centre as possible
Do NOT drill near electrical wires or plastic airlines
Subframe mounting plates: max 900mm apart
Mounting plates: min 50mm from any spring hanger bolt or rivet
Bolt centres: min 50mm from nearest hole
If any nearby hole >17mm — STOP and escalate to Supervisor (VSB Section H)
Use 14mm High Tensile 10.9 Bolts — holes in chassis max 1mm oversize
Hardened washer between chassis and nut — bolt unthreaded section must not contact chassis
Relocate rear OEM cross beam forward — drill new holes for 14mm 10.9 Bolts
Remove excess chassis length, allowing for bumper
Initial tank mounting — fit rubber or aluminium spacer per spec (rubber = full-length channel; aluminium = countersunk bolts)
Fabricate and fit front mounts — MUST have springs on bolts
If possible: fit foot valves and piping while vehicle is in the air
Fabricate and test-fit bumper
Measure, mark and drill: hydraulic tank holes
Measure, mark and drill: toolbox holes
Measure, mark and drill: pump frame holes
Measure, mark and drill: slops tin holes
Remove subframe for welding out — HOLD: do not proceed past this point until Supervisor review below
