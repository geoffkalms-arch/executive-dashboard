# Balanced Scorecard Initiatives — 2025-26
## Rationalised & Sharpened | June 2026

*Reduced from 67 initiatives to 36. Every initiative is tied to a Strategic Objective, has a named owner, and is achievable this year.*

---

## Context — Why We Rationalised

With the acquisition of new business and new people in new roles, a focused initiative list is essential. When everything is a priority, nothing is. The original 67 initiatives were the right instincts from the right people — but distributed across 12 departments they risked dispersing effort without landing outcomes.

**The filter applied to every initiative:**
1. Does it directly serve one of our three Strategic Objectives?
2. Does it have a single named owner?
3. Can it be meaningfully completed or progressed this year?

If the answer to any of these was no — it was deferred, merged, or removed.

## INTERNAL PROCESS PERSPECTIVE

*Initiatives that fix how we work.*

| # | Initiative | Owner | Strategic Objective | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IP1 | **Engineering Release Gate — Project Management** *(formerly: Implement Project Management)* | MA | First Pass | **Sharpened:** Not a generic PM tool rollout. Specific deliverables: (1) Weekly engineering throughput review — jobs in, jobs completed, jobs at risk; (2) Job-level milestones from intake to release; (3) Mandatory release checklist before package leaves engineering — BOM complete, SolidWorks sub-assembly interference check, peer drawing review, engineering lead sign-off. KPI: engineering on-time release rate — target 85% within 6 months. |
| IP2 | **Root Cause Closure Loop** *(formerly: Post Works Review)* | MA / AS | First Pass | **Sharpened:** Renamed and redefined. Not a periodic review — a mandatory closure requirement. Every repair action closed must have: root cause category, root cause owner, and corrective action documented. Monthly root cause report to management. Target: 100% closure rate within 3 months. This is what converts the repair system from a fix register into a learning system. |
| IP3 | Create robust inventory systems | GK | First Pass | Materials not available at build start is a top-5 throughput killer. Define stock items including pipework for aviation. Establish reorder points. Link to C1. |
| IP4 | Long lead items confirmed at job intake | GP | First Pass | Before a job enters the engineering queue, long lead items are identified and ordered. No build should stall waiting for a part that was known at intake. |
| IP5 | **Handover standard — Data to next person** *(formerly: Data to next person)* | GP | First Pass | **Sharpened:** Formalise the handover at every stage: Sales → Engineering, Engineering → Production, Production → Quality, Quality → Dispatch. Define: what data is handed over, in what format, with confirmed receipt. Sales → Engineering is already good — target excellent. Engineering → Production is the critical gap. |
| IP6 | **In-house scissor-lift platform manufacture** *(formerly: Scissors in house)* | MA | Throughput | Aviation dispenser builds require scissor-lift access platforms. Manufacturing these in-house removes external supply dependency and reduces build cycle time. Nearly ready to commence manufacture. Track: cost vs external supply, lead time reduction. |
| IP7 | Streamline manufacturing processes — reduce production cycle time | JW / JC | Throughput | Baseline current cycle time by product type (refueller, dispenser, tanker). Set reduction target. Identify top 3 cycle time constraints and eliminate them. Link to IP1 and IP5. |
| IP8 | Develop key process guides | AS | First Pass | Document the "one best way" for critical processes — engineering release, BOM completion, fabrication sign-off, quality inspection, dispatch checklist. Priority: engineering and production processes first. Safety contributes. Quality leads. |
| IP9 | Planning and scheduling — next level | RK | Throughput | Define what "next level" means: production schedule visible to all, updated daily, sequenced against engineering release dates and materials availability. Link to IP1 and IP3. |
| IP10 | Organisational clarity — map process flow and costing | JM | Throughput | Map the current costing and allocation process. Identify where it waits. Target: 40% reduction in costing turnaround time within 6 months. Determine if a systems investment is required. |
| IP11 | Parts sales — increase marketing and throughput | HS | Hunting | Revenue from parts sales is a margin-rich, low-capital-intensity revenue stream. Define target, build a go-to-market approach. |
| IP12 | AI in Leadership | GK (sponsor) | Throughput | Leaders using AI tools to accelerate decision-making, analysis, and communication. Proof of concept already demonstrated. Build capability across the leadership team. Share use cases internally. |

**Deferred from Internal Process:** ISO 9001 across business — 3-year journey, lay the foundation this year but don't make it a year-1 KPI. Optimise Margins LEAN (General Fuels) — merged and deferred.
