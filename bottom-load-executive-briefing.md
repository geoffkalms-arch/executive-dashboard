## The Issue in One Paragraph

[Customer Name]'s current road tanker loading facility uses top-fill loading for diesel and unleaded petrol.
Every fill operation simultaneously creates four uncontrolled safety hazards: the risk of tanker overfill,
the release of flammable and toxic petrol vapours directly to atmosphere, the risk of electrostatic sparks
igniting those vapours, and the requirement for drivers to work at 2.7–3.5 m height every time they load.
All four hazards are rated **EXTREME** under the Australian Standard risk matrix. The facility is
non-compliant with AS 1940:2017, the Work Health and Safety Act 2011, and at risk under state dangerous
goods and environmental legislation. The tankers are already set up for bottom fill. The solution is
available, proven, and straightforward.

---

## The Four Hazards — At a Glance

| Hazard | Current Risk | After Upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| **Overfill** | EXTREME — operator vigilance only, no auto shut-off | LOW — automatic overfill prevention valve + electronic interlock |
| **Vapour release** | EXTREME — open hatch, continuous venting to atmosphere every fill | LOW — closed system, 95%+ vapour captured and recovered |
| **Electrostatic spark** | EXTREME — manual bonding cable, no interlock, often skipped | LOW — coded electronic grounding verification with pump interlock |
| **Working at height** | EXTREME — 2.7–3.5 m access required every fill, every day | LOW — all operations at ground level, fall hazard eliminated |

## What Goes In

A **Bottom Load Skid** is a ground-level loading station that connects to the tanker from below via a
sealed, dry-break API coupler. The system includes:

- **API dry-break bottom-fill coupler** — sealed connection, no spill on disconnect
- **Automatic overfill prevention** — product stops flowing when the compartment is full, automatically
- **Vapour recovery system (VRS)** — displaced vapours return to the storage tank as liquid, not to air
- **Electronic grounding verification** — product cannot flow unless the tanker is confirmed grounded; monitored continuously
- **Preset metered delivery** — accurate volume control and stock reconciliation
- **Ground-level emergency stop** — immediate, accessible, at hand

---

## Regulatory Position

The current system is **non-compliant** with:
- **AS 1940:2017 Cl. 5.5** — requires a grounding interlock for tanker loading (a manual cable is not sufficient)
- **WHS Act 2011 s.19** — requires elimination of foreseeable risks where reasonably practicable
- **WHS Regulations 2017 Reg. 78** — working at height controls (handrails alone may not satisfy this)

An audit, a near-miss, or an insurance review could trigger enforcement action under the current setup.
